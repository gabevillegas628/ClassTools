Classroom Tools

Timers, displays and data tools for the lab and lecture hall. Each one runs in the browser and works full screen on a projector.

Exam Room Clock

Countdown, current time and end time for exams, with warnings and on-screen notes.

Practical Station Timer

Timed rotations for lab practicals, with move time between stations and progress tracking.

Distribution Inspector

Histogram and summary stats for a column of scores from a CSV, with a normal-curve overlay and PNG export.
Settings are saved in this browser only.
