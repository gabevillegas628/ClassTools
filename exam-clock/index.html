      Warnings show a banner and play a soft chime. The countdown turns amber at the second-to-last warning and red at the last one. If the page reloads during an exam, the clock picks up where it was.

      When time is up, a button for each after-exam timer appears under the clock, plus an "Other" box for a one-off timer. Space starts the next one, and pauses or resumes it. + / − adjust whichever timer is running.
